# Credits

## Photos

The six photos bundled in this folder are the user's own photos, supplied
by the user for PALM CITY — Cover Art Lab and used as the studio's
built-in sample set:

- `corvette-sunset.jpg` — red convertible on a palm-lined street at sunset (OCEAN DRIVE)
- `woman-convertible.jpg` — portrait in a convertible on a boulevard (PALM BOULEVARD)
- `yacht-aerial.jpg` — aerial view of a yacht in turquoise water (MARINA BAY)
- `bike-neon.jpg` — motorcycle under neon lights (NEON STRIP)
- `gas-station.jpg` — gas station glowing at night (HIGHWAY 95)
- `man-neon.jpg` — portrait under neon city lights (DOWNTOWN)

All six ship 100% offline inside the app. Photos the user uploads in the
studio are read on their own machine only and are never uploaded or shared.

No Rockstar Games artwork, logo, wordmark, character, or font is used.
No third-party photos are used.

## Fonts

- **Anton** — the heavy condensed face used for the main cover title.
  SIL Open Font License 1.1. Bundled locally in `app/fonts/`.
- **Yellowtail** — the neon script face used for the subtitle.
  SIL Open Font License 1.1. Bundled locally in `app/fonts/`.

Both fonts load from local files at runtime; the app makes no network
requests for them.
